## What to take from each

Anatomy. The object is the explanation. A fresher recognises a machine, not a diagram. Two failures seen in this course: too abstract (stacked plates, floating slabs, labelled boxes) and too flat (one slab, a few lines, the chapter's words on the faces). Build the parts that machine has, in groups, before geometry: base (plate, feet, screws, a scale), structure (rails, stops), mechanism (the one moving subject, its bushings), and the subject the model measures. A foot or a screw is part of the instrument. A part that invents a second story is not.

UI/UX, checked 2026-10-28. Use only these hits. Animate one or two elements; the rest stays still. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keyboard focus stays at least partly visible (WCAG 2.2 AA): no sticky bar covers the focused control. In Next.js the page stays a server component; the figure that moves is a client leaf.

Dimensional drawing. Black line on white, grays only, things told apart by line weight. The course page already follows that. Do not redraw a chapter as an empty-slot spec sheet.

## What not to take

- Anatomy's part quota as a target to hit. Forty unrelated parts is the labelled-box failure again.
- Anatomy's example objects (plotter, lock, arcade cabinet). Copy the craft.
- A palette, a display font, Phosphor icons, or GSAP from ui-ux-pro-max. The host is monochrome and the course does not load an animation library.
- Dimensional drawing's rule that a sheet shows slots instead of real content. A lesson shows the real step.

## Before geometry

Write the figure's question in the learner's words, then the object in one sentence, then the parts in the four groups. The moving mark equals one model field. The live readout names the state the model just entered, in words, beside the drawing. At most one tiny engraved label on the metal, and it is a unit or a nameplate, not the essay. If the only way to tell the stations apart is a word painted on each, the stations are the wrong shape or the readout is missing. A caption that translates shapes ("a drum means the lookup") is the same failure. Cover that caption and say the lesson aloud. If you cannot, change the object before you add another part. The machine comes before the glossary.
