Recruiters and leads need “who / why care” in under five seconds, then a ride that earns the scroll. Facts only from `src/content/site.ts`. City = La Paz; landscapes = five lesser-known Earth forms.

## Art direction

**Palette** — Cool moonlight base; warm window spill only in La Paz. Project hues from `site.ts` (green / red / blue / ink / amber) on portals, title flashes, brief blooms — never full-scene washes. Horizon air matches ground mist.

**Light** — Filmic night. Shared haze + valley mist. Ship search beam as the active ground light on land. Aurora on ice; blackbody lava on Erta Ale; dawn crescent + ISS at finale.

**Camera** — Ship ~62–70% width on desktop; bank with steer; hold-to-boost. Directed beats over constant glide: short holds into portals, punch on jump, crane-out at `200 OK`. Reduced motion: straight cuts, no roll/shake/fringing.

**Assets** — Keep ship, portal, Osaka scan. DEM from AWS Terrain Tiles for La Paz bowl, Illimani, and the five landscape horizons. Improve framing, grade, mist, portal marquees, per-place AIR.

**Data (hard)** — Aleph may supply **OSM + AWS Terrain only**. No Google Street View, satellite, or Google 3D meshes. La Paz footprints: Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints (CDLA-Permissive-2.0, quadkey 210031333); heights stylised. Google Open Buildings v3 (CC BY 4.0) remains an allowed alternative.

## What NOT to do

- Invent jobs, dates, metrics, or projects outside `site.ts`.
- Call the city Bengaluru or a hometown postcard.
- Swap the five forms for famous tourist icons.
- Use Google Street View / satellite / photoreal 3D tiles via Aleph or otherwise.
- Make every transition a portal-scale jump.
- Purple-glow cyber UI, hero dashboard clutter, lorem, filler adjectives.
- Break ≤120 kB first-load JS, CLS ~0, sound-before-opt-in, or WCAG AA.
