# Creative direction — La Paz + unique horizons

## Arc

Orbit (intrigue) → dive onto the Altiplano (discovery) → **La Paz amphitheater at night** with Illimani (signature) → project landscapes that are *not* the usual stock deserts and Alps (exploration) → sea and pole (escalation) → sunrise 200 OK (payoff / resolution).

## Why La Paz

Not Bengaluru (already in the HTML and on the resume). La Paz is a canyon capital: a bowl of light, walls of mid-rises, and a 6,000 m snow cone. It is immediately legible in one frame and rare on portfolio sites.

## Signature moments

1. Cloud deck breaks → canyon lights below, Illimani on the right.
2. Portal jump into a named landform (Chocolate Hills → Lençóis → Danxia → Roraima → Erta Ale).
3. Climb off the ice into sunrise with ISS silhouette.

## Art direction

Cooler, thinner night air over the city; warm sodium only in the valley floor; DEM rock graded for moonlight; preserve ship, portal, Osaka scan.

## Do not

- Google Street View / satellite / Earth meshes (Aleph ToS).
- Invent employment or claim the owner lives in La Paz.
- Replace the ship or portal.
- Purple-glass SaaS chrome.
