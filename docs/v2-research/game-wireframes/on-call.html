On Call: the 3D build

This greybox is 2D top-down so you can play the loop. The real build is the same game in 3D.

Camera

Three-quarter chase camera (35 mm), lower and closer on foot, lifts when you speed; slow-motion and a cut to a close shot when a mission passes.

World (free, CC0)

  • Kenney City Kit Roads, Commercial, Suburban and Industrial for streets, towers and the harbour.
  • Kenney Car Kit for your car and every request car.
  • Quaternius Modular Characters + Universal Animation Library when you step out on foot.
  • Kenney Interface and Impact sounds; radio beds from CC0 music.

The part that is hard to clone

  • Traffic is a live request simulation: every car is a request travelling client → CDN → gateway → service → cache / database and back. Thousands of them drawn as one instanced mesh.
  • An incident is a real fault in that sim: a slow cache turns traffic into a jam you can see, a bad deploy makes cars crash in red. Fixing it clears the jam.
  • Click any car to freeze time and see its trace drawn across the city, hop by hop with latency.
  • Day turns to night and the city lights up as severity rises.

Recruiter path

The phone is real HTML (résumé, projects, skills, contact) and opens before the 3D loads. "Résumé" is always one tap away. Nothing is locked behind a mission; missions add trophies, not access.

Phone (portrait)

The joystick steers by direction. Tap the map or a mission to set GPS, then Auto-drive follows the road graph. The phone fills the screen as a native-feeling app.

Performance

HTML phone paints first; the city streams in tiles (KTX2 textures, Meshopt geometry); one draw call for all traffic; target ≤ 3 MB before the first mission.
