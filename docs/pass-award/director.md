## Three signature moments (protect / amplify)

1. **Cloud-break into La Paz** — Deck clears to canyon city at night, Illimani pale on the far rim; Osaka scan stays downtown massing beside the path. First “real place” beat of the trip. Amplify: longer settle out of the cloud deck, bowl-side framing, Altiplano air (cool moonlight + warm spill + valley mist). Do not bury with HUD chrome or early portal energy.
2. **Portal as only loud cut** — Fly through the gateway window (`rings.entry`); project-colour flash; title into the panel; barrel roll. Quiet dips (city→first land, sea→ice) and air-only warp streaks protect that meaning. Horizon previews the next sky. Amplify: punch + hold into the ring; panel blur locked to the flash. Do not make every transition portal-scale.
3. **Sunrise departure** — Pull-up from ice, air cut, orbit at dawn; ISS silhouette; ship greets then engine glint to the sun; measured vitals under it, then contact. Amplify: dawn rim gold on the hull; credits in step with assets. Do not crowd the first sunrise frames with promo chips or invent vitals the browser cannot measure.

## Kill list (what NOT to add)

- **Purple-glow / neon cyberpunk UI** — no magenta blooms, grid overlays, or “hacker terminal” skins on the night film grade.
- **Hero / Wire dashboard clutter** — no stat strips, pill clusters, floating badges, schedule chips, or card grids over the poster or orbit. Impact stays Wire cards from `practice.impact` only.
- **Character-led bobblehead / likeness world** — Round trip locked; likeness needs owner approval (CLAUDE.md §10).
- **Invented copy** — no new employers, metrics, project outcomes, or filler adjectives outside `site.ts` / resume / LinkedIn / GitHub / owner.
- **Bengaluru-as-city / tourist swap** — city chapter is La Paz amphitheater; HTML location stays Bengaluru. Do not swap the five lesser-known forms for famous postcard icons.
- **Google SV / sat / photoreal 3D tiles** — Aleph = OSM + AWS Terrain only; footprints stay Microsoft Global ML (or allowed Open Buildings).
- **Portal inflation** — every hop is not a jump; quiet dips and air cuts stay quiet.
- **Full-scene project-colour washes** — hues live on portals, title flashes, brief blooms — not whole landscapes.
- **Sound / tilt before consent** — nothing plays before Sound on; iOS tilt only from Tilt.
- **Budget breaks** — first-load JS for `/` &gt; 120 kB, layout shift on panels, or WCAG AA regressions.
